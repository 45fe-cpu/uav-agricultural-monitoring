
# Preliminary NDVI Analysis Results

## Dataset
Multispectral UAV imagery of wheat crops collected in East Kazakhstan during the 2022 growing season.

Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7860751

## Analysis
Five multispectral images from experimental plot 10 were analyzed to examine temporal changes in NDVI.

Acquisition dates:
- May 18, 2022
- June 8, 2022
- June 21, 2022
- July 12, 2022
- July 25, 2022

## Outputs
- wheat_plot10_ndvi_maps.png
- wheat_plot10_ndvi_trend.png
- ndvi_plot10_summary.csv

## Limitations
This is a preliminary vegetation index analysis.

No independent ground-truth anomaly labels were available in the examined files. The analysis does not establish specific crop diseases or causes of vegetation stress.

The results require further validation before drawing agricultural conclusions.
